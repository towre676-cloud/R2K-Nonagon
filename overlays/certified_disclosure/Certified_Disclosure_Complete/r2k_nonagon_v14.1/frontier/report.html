2. Exact data cannot always identify the hidden machine

A three-mass path and a differently connected three-mass triangle have identical driving-point responses at every frequency. Both have unit masses, strictly positive grounding and inter-mass springs, positive-definite stiffness, and every mode visible at the observed port.

H(z) = (z² − 5z + 5) / (18 − 24z + 9z² − z³),   H(z)=e₁ᵀ(K−zI)⁻¹e₁.

One additional static displacement measurement distinguishes the displayed pair: 1/9 versus 19/234, an exact difference of 7/234. The construction extends to arbitrary order: even a complete transfer block on the first n−2 ports can remain identical after changing the hidden connections. No increase in numerical precision or frequency density at those unchanged ports resolves this exact ambiguity.

The constructive remedy uses known masses and full displacement coordinates at n distinct, nonresonant shifts:

K = (e₁1ᵀ + MXZ)X⁻¹.

Here the columns of X are measured displacement responses and Z contains the shifts. Under cyclicity, X is nonsingular and the unknown stiffness and graph are recovered. The single added sensor separates the constructed pairs; it is not a universal two-port reconstruction theorem. Validation: 257 exact physical network pairs and 6,684 checks, plus 56 checks in a separate determinant/adjugate verifier. The broader nonuniqueness principle is established realization theory; this is a checked constructive capability for the toolkit.

3. Independent continuous full-strip verification

A direct Arb contour program, importing no archived Taylor or DFT code, covers the entire rectangle 10 ≤ Im(s) ≤ 100, −0.1 ≤ Re(s) ≤ 1.1. All 45 adjoining tiles certify: 29 zeros, all simple and on the critical line, with no unresolved regions. Generation took 64.06 seconds; a fresh 45-tile replay independently reran this direct implementation and agreed throughout.

Each tile retains complete zero-free boundary partitions, moment enclosures and positive Hermite minors. Boundary interval midpoints and radii are stored as exact dyadics because a human-readable Arb string can deliberately coarsen a wide ball and lose its zero-exclusion information. Five analytic controls and seven ledger/trace corruptions exercise failure behavior; three serialization regressions check the repaired representation.

This independently implemented numerical route still trusts Arb's function evaluations and adaptive integrator. Static hashes and ledger checks alone do not prove the interval evaluations. The range is a known finite RH fact, not a new verification-height record or global proof; heights below 10 are not covered by this campaign. A separate attempted direct Lehmer calculation hit a 60-second budget and is retained as a limitation.

5. A reusable exact passive-model compiler

Z(s)=R₀+Ls+C⁻¹/s+Σ aⱼ/(1+τⱼs)+Σ bⱼτⱼs/(1+τⱼs).

For nonnegative coefficients and positive time constants, every term is analytic with nonnegative real part on Re(s)>0. The independent verifier interprets serialized decimal coefficients as exact rationals, checks the supported class and SI convention, and certifies this model-level property without a frequency grid. The compiler exports passive circuit realizations.

216 fitted models verified (24 development plus 192 confirmation task models, not 216 devices). The expanded RC/RL verifier passes 150 fixtures, 2,400 exact checks and 29 adversarial rejections, including optimized-Python attacks. RL relaxations permit real-part upturns while retaining the positive-real guarantee. All 216 fitted models contain zero RL terms: the new representation is mathematically checked, and its fitting accuracy remains untested on new protected data. Circuit exports are algebraically checked, not simulator-tested.

The restricted certificate is sufficient, not necessary for all positive-real functions. It certifies the serialized model, not battery behavior outside the observed range, a unique internal circuit, or hardware calibration. Ordinary Loewner fits frequently have right-half-plane poles despite low finite-band prediction error; prediction and structural validity are separate questions.

Numerical failure and evidence discipline

A zero-noise sanity check exposed a SciPy 1.17 automatic complex-tridiagonal solve failure on a well-conditioned 3×3 physical system. A separate literal-matrix reproducer shows a residual near 0.0017 versus machine precision with an explicit general solve; SciPy 1.18.1 does not reproduce that path defect. The affected initial adaptive experiment is invalidated and quarantined. The final experiment uses explicit general solves, residual checks and a separate NumPy solve comparison, followed by a fresh protocol freeze and untouched confirmation seed. The previous challenge was audited for this issue and left unchanged.

Protocol freezes are local, not external preregistration. The fitter is not supplied simulation truth; this is not external-custodian blinding. The battery study represents one batch, state of charge and instrument, not independent laboratories. Losses, timeouts, invalidated receipts, warnings and scope restrictions are included.
