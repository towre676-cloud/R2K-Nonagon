# Sources and relevance

The experiments implement standard local optimal-design formulas on the user's retained Nonagon model. They do not claim a full reproduction of each paper's algorithm or published benchmark.

1. Zhang, Fernando, Darouach. Functional Observability, Structural Functional Observability, and Optimal Sensor Placement. IEEE TAC 70(3), 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08923 . Mathematical observability and candidate-selection guarantees; no automatic transfer of its approximation ratio to the covariance heuristics here.
2. Montanari, Duan, Motter. On the Popov–Belevitch–Hautus tests for functional observability and output controllability. Automatica 174, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03245 . Dynamic functional-observability tests; not a static nonlinear global-injectivity theorem.
3. Attia, Leyffer, Munson. Robust A-Optimal Experimental Design for Sensor Placement in Bayesian Linear Inverse Problems. SIAM/ASA JUQ 13(2), 2025. https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1667543 . Worst-case uncertainty-model design. The new correlated selector is covariance-aware, not a reproduction of this paper's minimax optimization.
4. Neuberger, Alexanderian, van Bloemen Waanders. Goal oriented optimal design of infinite-dimensional Bayesian inverse problems using quadratic approximations. November 2024 preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07532 . Nonlinear target curvature; the current package implements first-order target design only, leaving its quadratic criterion as a subsequent comparison.
5. Katanić, Guo, Lygeros, Hug. Optimal PMU Placement for Kalman Filtering of DAE Power System Models. February 2025 manuscript. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03338 . Mixed-integer semidefinite benchmark for small dynamic systems. No dynamic MISDP reproduction is claimed here.
6. Das, Sharma, Pal. Linear State Estimation in Presence of Bounded Uncertainties: A Comparative Analysis. October 2025 preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.16693 . Interval and robust estimation under line-parameter error. Current perturbation tests are local sensitivity tests, not certified interval enclosures.
7. Moshtagh et al. Topology-Aware Graph Neural Network-based State Estimation for PMU-Unobservable Power Systems. 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03493 . Predictive IEEE-118 comparator for a different measurement/unknown model. Not trained or compared head-to-head in this package.
8. Ding et al. Power System Robust State Estimation As a Layer. June 2026 revision. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22836 . Optimization-embedded learned estimation; not implemented here.
9. PowerBench dataset, 2025: https://zenodo.org/records/15400564 . Code: https://github.com/RoshniAnna/PowerBench . External simulated distribution-network transfer benchmark, not run here.
10. Wu, Chen, Ghattas. Efficient goal-oriented linear Bayesian optimal experimental design. https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06627 . Older low-rank/offline-online computational baseline.
